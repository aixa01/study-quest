What is ?

is an application that provides a gamified STEM learning experience. You explore a world where you earn XP, level up, and collect pet companions as you learn.

Who is this for?

was made with students in mind, but anyone can use it! Learning can sometimes feel challenging, and research suggests that learning actively through gamification can make the process easier.

Growing up, and even in college, I struggled with learning certain topics and making them stick. is meant for supplementary learning alongside your main studies, to reinforce what you already know or introduce something new.

How it was built

Tech stack: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Vite, Firebase Authentication (Google sign-in), Cloud Firestore, and the Wolfram|Alpha API.

was built independently during HackPrinceton Fall 2026.
